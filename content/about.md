Hi, I’m Warren, and I’m a backend engineer who loves building things that work well, scale smoothly, and make people’s lives easier. Over the past eight years, I’ve worked on a mix of exciting projects where I’ve used tools like Go, Python, AWS, and GraphQL to create reliable and efficient systems. I really enjoy the challenge of solving tough problems and always learning something new, whether that’s diving into a new tech stack or finding better ways to optimize code. What excites me most is working with people who are just as passionate about solving problems as I am, and I always look forward to learning and growing from those experiences.

When I’m not deep in code, I’m usually making music or spending time with my dog. I’m a huge fan of playing bass, and I can easily lose myself in a good jam session or discovering new tunes. Outside of that, I try to stay active and enjoy time at the gym. I believe in balancing my work and passions because that’s what keeps me grounded and motivated. This blog is a little corner where I get to share both my professional journey and the things that make me tick. I hope you enjoy reading about the things I care about as much as I enjoy talking about them.
